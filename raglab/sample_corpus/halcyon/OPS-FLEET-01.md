# Operations: Carrier Performance Review

## Summary
On-time delivery for standard parcels was 91% in H1 2025. Express metro parcels achieved 97%. International lanes averaged 11 business days, within the published 7 to 14 day estimate.

## Issues
Regional Western Australia lanes averaged 8 business days, above the 6-day upper estimate. The ETA shown at checkout for those postcodes has been widened to 4 to 8 business days.

## Return logistics
Return shipping labels are now generated by the carrier API directly, cutting label errors from 3% to under 0.5%.
