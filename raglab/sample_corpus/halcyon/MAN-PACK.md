# Meridian 45 Pack — Fit & Care Guide

## Fitting the hipbelt
Load the pack with 8 to 10 kg, loosen all straps, then tighten the hipbelt so the padding sits over the top of the hip bones. Tighten the shoulder straps until the pack sits flush, then pull the load lifters to about 45 degrees.

## Torso adjustment
Slide the harness ladder to match torso length. The Meridian 45 fits torsos from 43 to 53 cm.

## Cleaning
Hand wash with lukewarm water and mild soap. Never machine wash or tumble dry. Air dry away from direct sunlight.

## Guarantee
Packs are covered by our lifetime workmanship guarantee. Wear from normal use is not a defect.
