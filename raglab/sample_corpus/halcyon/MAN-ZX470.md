# ZX-470 Cairn Headlamp — User Manual

## Overview
The ZX-470 Cairn is the predecessor of the Ridgeline. It delivers 600 lumens on high and uses three AAA batteries. It is rated IPX4 (splash resistant) and is not suitable for submersion.

## Modes
Press once for high, twice for low, three times for strobe. Hold for two seconds for red mode.

## Battery replacement
Slide the rear cover down and replace all three cells at once. Do not mix old and new cells. The Cairn does not support rechargeable USB charging.
