# Ember 20 Power Bank — Safety & Use

## Capacity
The Ember 20 stores 20,000 mAh (74 Wh) and is permitted in carry-on luggage on most airlines. Check with your carrier before flying.

## Charging devices
Two USB-C ports deliver up to 45 W combined. Pass-through charging is supported but reduces cell longevity.

## Safety
Do not expose to temperatures above 45 °C. Discontinue use if the casing swells. Battery cells are covered by the 12-month battery warranty described in the Limited Warranty Terms.
