# ZX-481 Ridgeline Headlamp — User Manual

## Overview
The ZX-481 Ridgeline headlamp (often called simply "the 481") delivers 900 lumens on boost and 120 hours on the reserve setting. It is rated IPX7 for water resistance: submersion to one metre for 30 minutes.

## Charging the battery
Charge the removable 3400 mAh lithium-ion cell with the supplied USB-C cable. A full charge takes about 3.5 hours. The indicator pulses amber while charging and turns solid green when complete. Do not charge below 0 °C.

## Modes
Press once for low, twice for medium, three times for high. Hold for two seconds to enter red night mode. Double-click from off to activate boost for 30 seconds.

## Lock-out
Hold both buttons for four seconds to lock the headlamp for transport. The indicator flashes red three times when locked.

## Battery care
Store at 40 to 60 percent charge for long periods. Battery capacity below 70% of rated capacity within the first 12 months is covered under warranty; see the Limited Warranty Terms.
