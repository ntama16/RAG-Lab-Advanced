# Price Match & Promotions Policy

## Price match
We match the advertised price of an identical in-stock item from an authorised Australian retailer within 14 days of purchase. Marketplace sellers, clearance and refurbished listings are excluded.

## Post-purchase price drops
If we reduce the price of an item within 14 days of your purchase, contact support for a credit of the difference.

## Stacking
Promotional codes cannot be combined with price-match adjustments or Final Sale pricing.
