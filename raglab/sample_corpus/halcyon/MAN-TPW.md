# TrailPulse Watch — Quick Start

## Pairing
Open the TrailPulse app, enable Bluetooth and hold the crown for five seconds until the watch shows a pairing code. Enter the code in the app.

## GPS acquisition
First GPS fix may take up to two minutes outdoors with a clear view of the sky. Subsequent fixes take under 15 seconds thanks to cached ephemeris data, which is refreshed each time the watch syncs.

## Battery life
Smartwatch mode: 14 days. Continuous GPS: 36 hours. Expedition mode (GPS every 60 seconds): 120 hours.

## Water rating
The TrailPulse is rated 5 ATM. It is suitable for swimming but not for diving or high-pressure water jets.

## Firmware
Firmware updates are installed from the app while the watch is charging and above 50% battery.
