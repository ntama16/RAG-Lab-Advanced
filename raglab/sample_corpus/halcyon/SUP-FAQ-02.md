# Customer FAQ: Headlamps

## My Ridgeline won't turn on out of the box
It is probably in transport lock-out. Hold both buttons for four seconds; the indicator flashes red three times and the lamp unlocks.

## How long does the 481 take to charge?
About 3.5 hours from empty with the supplied USB-C cable. The light turns solid green when full.

## Can I swim with my headlamp?
The ZX-481 is IPX7: it survives submersion to one metre for 30 minutes, but it is not designed for swimming or diving. The older ZX-470 Cairn is only splash resistant.

## Is the battery covered by warranty?
Yes, for 12 months, if capacity drops below 70% of rated capacity.
