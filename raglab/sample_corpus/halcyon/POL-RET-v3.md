# Returns & Exchanges Policy (v3, current)

## Return window
Apparel, footwear, packs and other soft goods may be returned within 45 days of delivery. Electronics — headlamps, GPS beacons, power banks and the TrailPulse watch — must be returned within 21 days of delivery. The window is counted from the carrier's delivery scan, not the order date.

## Condition
Products must be unused and in resalable condition. Electronics must include all accessories and the original box. Opened electronics are accepted within the 21-day window but may incur a 10% restocking fee if the seal is broken.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the warehouse receiving the item. Bank posting may take a further 3 to 5 business days.

## Exchanges
Exchanges for size or colour remain free. Cross-product exchanges are processed as a return plus a new order.

## Final sale and gift cards
Final Sale items and gift cards are non-refundable. Gift returns are refunded as store credit.
