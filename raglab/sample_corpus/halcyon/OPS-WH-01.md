# Warehouse SOP: Inbound Returns

## Receiving
Scan the return label on arrival. Parcels without an RMA number or return reference are set aside and the sender is contacted within 2 business days.

## Inspection
Inspect soft goods for wear, odour and tag presence. Test electronics for power-on and, for headlamps, run a capacity check at 0.5 C discharge. Record the result in the service desk within 3 business days of receipt.

## Disposition
Resalable goods return to stock. Defective goods go to the engineering quality bin. Non-resalable but functional goods are routed to the refurbished outlet.

## Refund trigger
Approving the inspection record triggers the refund automatically. Refunds reach the customer's payment method within 5 business days.
