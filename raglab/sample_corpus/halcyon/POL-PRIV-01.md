# Customer Data & Privacy Notice

## What we collect
We collect name, delivery address, email, order history and device telemetry that customers opt into through the TrailPulse app.

## Retention
Order records are retained for seven years for tax purposes. Telemetry is retained for 24 months, then aggregated and anonymised.

## Access and deletion
Customers can request an export or deletion of their personal data via the account page. Deletion requests are fulfilled within 30 days except where records must be retained by law.
