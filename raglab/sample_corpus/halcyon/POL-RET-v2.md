# Returns & Exchanges Policy (v2, superseded)

## Return window
Customers may return any unused product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Items must be in original packaging with tags attached.

## Electronics
Electronic products, including headlamps, GPS beacons and the TrailPulse watch, follow the same 30-day window as apparel and hard goods.

## Exchanges
Exchanges for a different size or colour are free of charge. A prepaid return shipping label is generated from the order page. The replacement ships once the carrier scans the return.

## Final sale
Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout cannot be returned or exchanged.
