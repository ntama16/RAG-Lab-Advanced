# Finance: Refund Accounting Procedure

## Recognition
Refunds are recognised in the period the warehouse approves the inspection record, not when the customer initiates the return.

## Restocking fees
The 10% restocking fee on opened electronics is recorded as other income. It is waived automatically for warranty and DOA cases.

## Reconciliation
Payment-gateway refund batches are reconciled daily. Discrepancies above $500 are escalated to the finance lead within one business day.
