# Shipping & Delivery Estimates

## Processing time
Orders placed before 2 pm AEST on a business day are dispatched the same day. Otherwise dispatch occurs the next business day. Personalised items add two business days.

## Estimated time of arrival
Standard delivery within Australia: 3 to 6 business days after dispatch. Express: 1 to 2 business days to metro areas. International orders: 7 to 14 business days depending on customs. The ETA shown at checkout is an estimate, not a guarantee.

## Tracking
A tracking link is emailed at dispatch. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to update after the first carrier scan.

## Lost parcels
A parcel is considered lost if tracking shows no movement for 10 business days. Contact support to open a carrier investigation; a replacement or refund is issued once the carrier confirms the loss.
