# Warehouse SOP: Refurbished Outlet Intake

## Eligibility
Non-resalable but functional goods are routed to the refurbished outlet after passing the inbound inspection.

## Inspection
Inspect soft goods for wear, odour and tag presence. Test electronics for power-on and, for headlamps, run a capacity check at 0.5 C discharge. Record the result in the service desk within 3 business days of receipt. Units that fail are returned to the engineering quality bin.

## Grading
Grade A: cosmetically perfect, open box. Grade B: light cosmetic marks. Grade C: visible wear, fully functional. Grade C items are sold with a 6-month warranty instead of the standard two years.

## Labelling
Each refurbished unit receives a new serial sticker prefixed "R-" so warranty lookups resolve to the refurbished terms.
