# Customer FAQ: Orders and Returns

## How long do I have to send something back?
Soft goods such as jackets, packs and boots can be sent back within 45 days of delivery. Electronics such as headlamps, beacons and watches must be sent back within 21 days. See the current Returns & Exchanges Policy for details.

## Can I return a gift?
Yes. Gift returns are refunded as store credit to the recipient.

## When will I get my money back?
Refunds are processed within 5 business days of the warehouse receiving your return.

## I ordered the wrong size
Start a free size exchange from your order page. The replacement ships once the carrier scans your return.
