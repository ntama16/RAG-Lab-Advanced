# Finance Memo: Return on Marketing Spend, FY25

## Headline
Return on marketing spend was 3.2x in FY25, up from 2.7x. Paid search delivered the strongest return at 4.1x; social video returned 1.9x.

## Method
Return is calculated as incremental gross margin divided by channel spend, using a 21-day attribution window.

## Recommendation
Shift 15% of the social video budget to paid search for the spring campaign and review returns after one quarter.
