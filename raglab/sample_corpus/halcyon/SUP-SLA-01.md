# Support Service Levels

## Response targets
Email tickets: first response within 1 business day. Live chat: within 5 minutes during support hours (8 am to 8 pm AEST, Monday to Saturday). Warranty escalations: resolution proposal within 5 business days.

## Priority
DOA reports and safety concerns (swelling batteries, overheating) are priority one and are answered within 2 hours during support hours.

## Language
Support is offered in English, with machine-assisted translation for other languages.
