# Support Playbook: Dead-on-Arrival Units

## Definition
A unit is dead-on-arrival (DOA) when it fails to power on or shows a hardware fault within 7 days of delivery.

## Handling
DOA units bypass the standard inspection queue. Dispatch a replacement immediately with express shipping and issue a prepaid label for the faulty unit. Do not ask the customer to troubleshoot beyond a single charge cycle.

## Common false positives
Headlamps shipped in transport lock-out mode appear dead. Ask the customer to hold both buttons for four seconds before declaring a ZX-481 DOA. TrailPulse watches ship in deep-sleep mode and wake after 10 seconds on the charger.
