# Limited Warranty Terms

## Coverage
Halcyon Trailworks warrants hard goods and electronics against defects in materials and workmanship for two years from the date of purchase. Apparel and packs carry a lifetime workmanship guarantee.

## Exclusions
The warranty does not cover normal wear, damage from misuse, unauthorised modification, water damage beyond the product's stated rating, or consumables. Rechargeable battery cells are covered for 12 months and are considered defective only when capacity falls below 70% of rated capacity within that period.

## Making a claim
Warranty claims are opened through the Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) process. Customers must provide proof of purchase, photographs of the defect and the product serial number. An RMA number must be written on the outside of the parcel; parcels without one are returned unopened.

## Remedies
At our discretion we repair, replace with an equivalent product, or refund the purchase price. Replacements carry the remainder of the original warranty period.
