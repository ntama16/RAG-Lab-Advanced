# Support Playbook: Opening an RMA

## When to open an RMA
Open a Return Merchandise Authorization when a customer reports a defect, a dead-on-arrival (DOA) unit, or a warranty issue. Change-of-mind returns do not need an RMA and go through the standard returns flow.

## Steps
1. Verify proof of purchase and confirm the product is within the warranty period.
2. Ask for photos or a short video of the fault and the serial number.
3. Create the RMA in the service desk; the system emails the customer an RMA number and a prepaid label.
4. Remind the customer to write the RMA number on the outside of the box.

## Turnaround
Warehouse inspection takes 3 business days from receipt. The customer receives the outcome by email. Replacement units are dispatched with express shipping at no charge.

## Escalation
Repeated failures of the same product line are flagged to Engineering through the quality channel.
